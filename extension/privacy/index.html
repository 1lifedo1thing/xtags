Read the post.
See its intent.

Xtags adds AI-estimated intent labels and probabilities beside posts on X, with optional signals for rage bait, machine-generated text and undisclosed advertising.

Requires Chrome 140+ and an API key for the selected service. TypeSafe is the default; compatible custom HTTPS APIs are supported. After consent, extracted post text and author handles are sent to the selected service for classification. Collapsed long posts use their full text before expansion. Provider charges may apply. Predictions can be wrong.

One purpose

Label posts you browse. Set signal thresholds, pause processing, skip replies and reuse cached judgments.

Two languages

Follow Chrome's UI language, or choose English or Chinese in the popup. Switching language does not repeat classification requests.
