Xtags — AI Content Filter for X

Spot suspected AI posts. Keep your feed focused.
Xtags estimates whether text posts on X / Twitter were primarily drafted or substantially rewritten by AI. Posts at or above your threshold automatically collapse after analysis, retaining their author, timestamp and labels. The AI indicator follows existing labels inline. Click the underlined Expand post button to read the content.

Choose how to filter
• Auto-collapse: folds the body, media and actions after analysis. Expanded posts keep a light red background and can be collapsed again.
• Light red background: keep the content visible with a subtle tint.
• Label only: show an estimate without changing the body display.
• Adjust an independent AI threshold, initially 0.85. Mode and threshold changes reuse existing judgments without repeating API requests.
• Manual expansion is remembered for the current tab; detail pages stay open.

Long posts, analyzed before expansion
Xtags uses the full text already available in X's page data before you click Show more. If unavailable, it waits or retries instead of treating the preview as the entire post.

Intent signals, too
Retains Inform, Persuade, Provoke, Sell, Entertain and Other with probabilities, plus rage-bait and undisclosed-promotion signals. Estimates textual cues without verifying payments, partnerships or factual claims.

Setup and costs
Requires Chrome 140+, internet access and your own selected-provider API key. Uses TypeSafe's Jev by default; custom HTTPS services must support the TypeSafe System One format. Provider charges may apply.
1. Open Settings. Keep the default provider or save a compatible full HTTPS API URL and grant host access.
2. Read the data notice and privacy policy, actively check the acknowledgement, click Agree and enable, then enter that service's key.
3. Enable Xtags and refresh X. Choose your display mode and AI threshold.
Automatically follows Chrome's UI language or lets you choose English / Simplified Chinese. Pause processing, skip replies, clear the cache or retry failed posts.

Data and limitations
Analysis is not on-device. After consent and activation, text and author handles from rendered posts go directly to the selected API. This can include posts outside the viewport and protected posts you can access. Collapsed long posts send available full text before expansion. API keys are accessible only to trusted extension pages and the background worker; content scripts receive a key-present flag. Keys, settings and a bounded judgment cache stay in local browser storage; the persistent cache excludes raw post text. The developer has no classification or telemetry server. The provider receives normal connection information such as your IP address.

AI estimates can be wrong. Scores are model estimates, not validated detection accuracy; 0.85 is an initial display threshold. Labels cannot prove authorship or establish falsehood or harmful intent. Text only: no image or video analysis. Folding happens after a judgment arrives. Xtags is independent and not affiliated with X Corp.
