Spot suspected AI posts.
Keep your feed focused.

Identify suspected AI-generated text on X / Twitter. Automatically collapse posts after analysis, use a light red highlight, or show labels only. Expand any time.

Your filter, your choice

Independent AI threshold, inline indicators and an underlined expand button. Manual expansion is remembered in this tab; detail pages stay open.

Long posts, too

Analyze full text already in X's page data before expanding. Intent, rage-bait and undisclosed-promotion signals remain available.

Requires Chrome 140+ and your selected provider's API key. TypeSafe is the default; compatible custom HTTPS APIs are supported. After consent, post text and author handles are sent to that provider, including full text of collapsed long posts. Analysis is not on-device. Provider charges may apply. Estimates can be wrong and do not prove authorship; scores are not measured accuracy.

识别疑似 AI 推文，减少阅读干扰

自动折叠、仅微红提示或仅标签，过滤程度由你决定。AI 提示和无框下划线展开按钮紧随原有标签；详情页默认展开。长推文在手动展开前使用页面已有全文判断。保留六类主意图及风险信号，帮助你少被带节奏。

需要 Chrome 140+、自备 API key；同意后正文和作者账号发送至所选服务，可能收费。模型分数不是实测识别准确率，不能证明文本来源。
